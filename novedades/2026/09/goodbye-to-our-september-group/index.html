Goodbye to our September group

September 15, 2026

Ten days, eleven travellers and a farewell night full of music: our September group has returned home.

Highlights included a full day with the para-ecologists checking camera traps, a pottery afternoon with the women of the community and a minga in which everyone helped to plant the new agroforestry plot.

Thank you for your openness, your curiosity and your help. The next tour starts in January.
