The Shuar

The Shuar are one of the largest Indigenous peoples of the Amazon. Their language is Shuar Chicham. Their life is deeply connected to the forest, the rivers and the family garden (aja), which provides food, medicine, materials and knowledge passed down from generation to generation.

The community of Maikiuants surrounded by rainforest

Maikiuants

Maikiuants is a Shuar community in the province of Morona Santiago in Ecuador’s southern Amazon, and part of the Pueblo Shuar Arutam. Surrounded by rainforest and clear rivers, the community combines traditional life with its own projects in plant nurseries, agroforestry and the protection of its territory.

The Kampuntin team with a group of visitors

Kampuntin

Kampuntin is the organisation that makes tours and volunteering in Maikiuants possible. We organise the journey, accompany the groups and make sure the income reaches the community and its projects. What is shared, when and how is decided by the community.
