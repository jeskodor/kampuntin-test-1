Volunteering in Maikiuants

We're looking for people who like to live together and in places far from the city. To come here, you have to be able to get out of your comfort zone. In return, living in an Indigenous community in the Amazon is a great learning experience.

Who are we looking for?

We're two and a half hours from the nearest amenities. You'll feel at home here if you…

  • enjoy being proactive and taking the initiative,
  • like working in the field, rain or shine,
  • want to learn about plants, agroforestry and permaculture,
  • enjoy living in a group and sharing tasks,
  • are open-minded, respectful and flexible.

How you'll help

We are mainly looking for people to support these tasks.

🌱

Plant nursery

Taking care of the nursery: preparing seeds, transplanting seedlings and watering.

🌳

Planting

Planting trees and crops on the community's plots.

🍌

Agroforestry

Maintaining the agroforestry crops by weeding, pruning and harvesting.

🔨

Construction

Helping with construction work and infrastructure improvements.

Life in the jea

We sleep in a traditional house called a jea, in the middle of the jungle. There are bunk beds on three levels and the possibility of hanging a hammock. The shower is the best in the world: a pure river with fresh water.

A well-equipped kitchen is available for cooking. A solar panel supplies it with electricity, and there is a blender and a juice extractor. In the future, we plan to install a small fridge for food storage. Meals are usually shared with the other volunteers.

  • 🛖 Traditional jea in the jungle
  • 🛏️ Three-level bunk beds or hammock
  • 🏞️ Pure river as your shower
  • ☀️ Solar-powered kitchen
  • 🥤 Blender and juice extractor
  • 🧊 Small fridge (planned)
The traditional jea in the middle of the rainforest

Free time

In your free time, you can make the most of the area: we can take you on beautiful walks, you can play with the children, take part in pottery-making with the women, make crafts, play music and sing.

It's a place of creativity and freedom where anything is possible.
